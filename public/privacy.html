Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

DM Lantern is a campaign manager for tabletop roleplaying games. This page explains what we collect when you use it, why we collect it and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We never store your actual password.
  • Campaign content: everything you or your table add to a campaign, such as sessions, notes, characters, maps, lore and uploaded images.
  • Sign in records: a session token so you stay signed in, and a short lived count of failed sign in attempts to protect accounts from guessing attacks.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps standard request logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and troubleshooting.

Cookies

We use one essential cookie to keep you signed in. It lasts up to 60 days or until you sign out. We use no advertising or tracking cookies today. If that changes, we will update this page and ask for consent where the law requires it.

How we use your information

  • To run your account and the campaigns you create or join.
  • To show players the content their DM shares with them.
  • To keep the service secure and fix problems.
  • To contact you about your account when needed.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who can see your content

Campaign content is visible to the members of that campaign according to their role. The DM sees the campaign's content. Players see what the DM shares, the party's shared notes and their own private notes. Private notes and private map marks are visible only to the player who made them.

Where your data is stored

DM Lantern runs on Cloudflare, which stores data in its global network. Web fonts load from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Keeping and deleting your data

A DM can delete a campaign at any time from Campaign Settings, which removes its content and images. To delete your whole account, email us from the address on your account and we will remove it within 30 days.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Email hello@dmlantern.com with any request or question.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page with a new date.
