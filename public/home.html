What is DM Lantern?

DM Lantern is a free, browser based campaign manager for tabletop roleplaying games. Dungeon Masters and Game Masters use it to prepare sessions, keep notes on characters, NPCs, locations, items and story threads, run combat and share handouts. Players join a campaign with an invite code and get their own view of everything the DM has revealed.

Features

Everything you need to run your campaign

One place for your prep, your world and your table, so you spend game night playing instead of searching through notes.

Session planner

Build each session as scenes and beats: read aloud text, encounters, NPC dialogue and secret notes. Tick beats off as you play.

Play mode for game night

A clean run sheet with a live table log, quick lookups for any NPC or place, and initiative and dice in a side drawer.

NPCs, places and items

A searchable record for every character, location and treasure, with secrets kept for your eyes only.

Story threads and choices

Follow every plot thread, the choices your players make and the consequences waiting for them.

Interactive world map

Upload your map, then add markers, routes, regions and labels. Choose exactly what the party can see.

Lore book and factions

Write your lore as a book that turns its pages, reveal entries as the party learns them and track reputation with every faction.

DM toolkit

Initiative tracker, dice roller, quick notes, secrets, party inventory, random tables and music links.

Session recaps

Send the party a recap and the details of the next game in a couple of clicks.

For players

Your players get their own companion

Players see the world as their characters know it. As you reveal people, places and lore, it appears in their view straight away.

  • One account for every campaign. Players sign up once and join any game with an invite code.
  • A codex of what they know. Every NPC, place and handout you share, all in one place.
  • A personal journal. Private notes for themselves and shared notes for the party.
  • A party tracker. Leads, quests and loot the whole table can update.
  • Private stays private. Players decide which notes and map marks the party can see.

FAQ

Questions DMs ask

Is DM Lantern free?

Yes. Creating an account, running campaigns and inviting players is free.

Which tabletop RPGs does DM Lantern work with?

Any of them. DM Lantern focuses on story, people and places, so it suits Dungeons & Dragons 5e, Pathfinder, Call of Cthulhu, Daggerheart, Warhammer roleplay games and homebrew systems alike.

Do my players need an account?

Yes, a free one. Each player signs up once with an email and password, then joins your campaign with the invite code you send. The same account works for every campaign they join.

Can I run more than one campaign?

Yes. Run as many campaigns as you like and play in other people's games from the same account. Each campaign keeps its own separate data.

Can the DM read players' private notes?

Private notes are visible only to the player who wrote them. Players choose which notes and map marks to share with the party.

Does DM Lantern work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser with layouts made for phones, tablets and laptops, and there is nothing to install.

Can I back up my campaign?

Yes. The DM can download a full backup of a campaign at any time from Campaign Settings.

How is DM Lantern different from a notes app or a wiki?

DM Lantern is built around running sessions: scene by scene plans, a play mode for game night, initiative and dice, and a player view that reveals lore and handouts as the story unfolds.
